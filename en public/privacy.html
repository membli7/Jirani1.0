This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data on the Jirani Chama platform (the "Platform"), in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (the "Act") and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021.

2. Personal data we collect

2.1 We collect the following categories of personal data:

  1. (a) Account and identity data: full name, nickname, Kenyan phone number, email address, role within a Chama, account status and profile photo (if you upload one);
  2. (b) Authentication data: a hashed PIN, session data stored in an HTTP-only cookie, PIN recovery data, and metadata about passkeys you register (credential identifiers and device information, but not any fingerprint image or biometric template);
  3. (c) Notification data: browser push subscription endpoint and keys, if you enable browser notifications;
  4. (d) Chama membership and financial data: membership and role history, contributions, penalties, welfare entries, share capital, loan applications, loan decisions, guarantor pledges, repayment records, dividend records and loan review findings;
  5. (e) Attendance and governance data: attendance records, meeting dates, officer election records (candidate selected, timestamp and voter token), and group chat messages;
  6. (f) Support data: name, email address, subject and message submitted through our contact form; and
  7. (g) Operational and audit data: audit log events, system logs, and records of actions taken in the Platform, including who recorded or changed an entry and when.

2.2 Much of this data is entered by you, by other members or by Chama officers. Some is generated by the Platform, including loan eligibility outcomes, repayment schedules, dividend calculations and summaries.

2.3 We do not knowingly collect data about children under 18, and accounts may not be created for minors. We do not collect biometric data or payment card details.

2.4 Sensitive personal data. The Platform is not designed to collect health, religious, political or other sensitive personal data as defined in section 2 of the Act. If a member enters such data in a free-text field, such as a chat message or a loan purpose, the Chama decides whether it is necessary and lawful to keep it.

3. How and why we use your data

3.1 We use personal data for the following purposes:

  1. (a) to create and operate your account and authenticate you;
  2. (b) to record and administer your Chama's membership, contributions, penalties, loans, repayments, dividends, attendance and elections;
  3. (c) to calculate loan eligibility, repayment schedules, penalties and dividends according to your Chama's rules;
  4. (d) to send you notifications about contributions due, loan repayments, meetings, elections and account security;
  5. (e) to respond to support requests;
  6. (f) to maintain security, detect and prevent fraud and misuse, and keep audit records; and
  7. (g) to comply with legal obligations, including tax and record-keeping obligations.

3.2 Lawful bases. We process personal data on the following lawful bases under section 30 of the Act:

  1. (a) Contract: to provide the Platform to you and your Chama;
  2. (b) Legal obligation: to keep records that Kenyan law requires;
  3. (c) Legitimate interests: to keep the Platform secure, prevent misuse and maintain audit records, where these interests are not overridden by your rights; and
  4. (d) Consent: for optional features you choose to enable, such as browser push notifications and profile photos. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

3.3 Automated decisions. The Platform makes automated loan eligibility decisions using the rules configured by your Chama. You may request human review as described in the Terms of Service. Human review findings are recorded and visible to the borrower.

3.4 Notifications. Browser notifications may include financial details, such as an instalment amount or due date. You may disable notifications in your profile settings.

4. Who sees your data

4.1 Your visibility within the Platform depends on your role, as set out in the Terms of Service. In summary:

  1. (a) you can see your own records;
  2. (b) other members see your name and a masked version of your phone number;
  3. (c) the Chairperson and Secretary see your full phone number;
  4. (d) authorised officers see group financial and loan records as permitted by the Chama's rules; and
  5. (e) audit logs are visible only to the Chairperson and the Treasurer.

4.2 Chama data is visible to the Chama. Financial and membership records held for a Chama are visible to its members and officers in accordance with the permissions in section 4.1.

4.3 Service providers. We share personal data with service providers only as needed to operate the Platform. These include hosting and database providers, email delivery providers, push notification services and cache and infrastructure services. Each provider is bound by a written contract requiring confidentiality, security and processing only on our instructions.

4.4 Legal requirements. We may disclose personal data where required by a court order, statute, or a lawful request from a regulator, including the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, the Kenya Revenue Authority or the Directorate of Criminal Investigations.

4.5 We do not sell personal data.

7. Your rights

7.1 Under the Act you have the right:

  1. (a) to be informed about how your data is used (this Policy);
  2. (b) to access your personal data;
  3. (c) to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  4. (d) to object to processing, including processing for direct marketing;
  5. (e) to request deletion of personal data where the Act permits;
  6. (f) to request portability of data you provided, where the Act permits; and
  7. (g) not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you, as described in Section 3.3.

7.2 How to exercise your rights. Send your request to [DPO EMAIL] or use the profile settings where the right is available there. Chama data requests may be directed to your Chama's Chairperson, who coordinates with us. We verify your identity before acting on a request and respond within [●] days, or within the period required by the Act if different.

7.3 Limits. Some rights are limited by law or by the Chama's record-keeping obligations. For example, we may keep financial records that a court, tax authority or regulator requires us to keep. Where we refuse a request, we tell you why.

7.4 Complaints. You may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you believe your rights have been infringed.

8. Security

8.1 We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, as required by section 41 of the Act, including:

  1. (a) PINs stored only as irreversible hashes;
  2. (b) session cookies that are HTTP-only and expire after inactivity;
  3. (c) rate limiting on login attempts;
  4. (d) passkey credential metadata, without storing any biometric template;
  5. (e) role-based access controls, as described in Section 4;
  6. (f) audit logging of sensitive actions; and
  7. (g) encryption of data in transit using HTTPS.

8.2 No system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your PIN, passkeys and devices secure.

8.3 Secret ballot. Officer elections are conducted as secret ballots. No user, including any Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer or other officer, can see how any individual member voted. Election records hold the candidate selected, a timestamp and a voter token, and results are shown only in aggregate.

9. Personal data breaches

9.1 If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a real risk of harm to you, we notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by section 43 of the Act.

9.2 Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we also notify you without undue delay, describing the breach, its likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed.

9.3 Where we act as data processor for a Chama, we notify the Chama without undue delay so that it can meet its own notification duties.

10. Cookies and local storage

10.1 The Platform uses an HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. This cookie is strictly necessary for the Platform to work.

10.2 If you enable browser notifications, the Platform stores a push subscription endpoint and encryption keys on your device. You can remove them at any time from your profile settings or your browser settings.

10.3 We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
