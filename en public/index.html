    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Jirani Chama?

    A modern financial management platform designed for Kenyan table-banking and savings groups to keep contributions, loans, and decisions in one secure space.

    How do I get my group started?

    Create your profile, invite members with one-time codes, and use scheduled secret-ballot elections for post-setup officer seats.

    Can we track individual contributions and balances?

    Members and officers can review recorded contributions, balances, loans, and repayments. The app records what the group entered; cash is received or paid outside it.

    Are our group's money and data secure?

    Access is role-scoped and key actions are recorded in an application audit log. The log is not tamper-evident; compare records with receipts and keep backups.

    How are monthly contributions split between welfare and share capital?

    The Chairperson sets the monthly welfare amount during setup. A contribution first settles outstanding penalties, then covers the member's welfare amount once per month; any remainder goes to share capital.

    How does the platform handle loan approvals and repayments?

    Each Chama sets its own thresholds. The app immediately checks active-membership period, recorded share-capital contributions, the amount tier, the requested amount against the Chama balance and the member’s existing loan exposure, and two guarantors’ available share capital. An eligible request is automatically approved without a Chairperson or Treasurer vote; authorized officers can inspect loan records, and the borrower may request a human review. Members can see the reducing-balance repayment estimate before submitting. The app records repayments but does not transfer or disburse funds.

    How are late-payment penalties assessed?

    A late-dated contribution can create the configured late penalty when the Treasurer records it. Separately, the Chairperson or Secretary must run the monthly assessment for eligible members who have not met the welfare threshold. That assessment is manual, not automatic.

    What does the month-end close review show?

    It summarizes recorded cash contributions and repayments, tracked open loan balances, and items officers may need to follow up, such as requests left open under the previous loan workflow or missing references. New loan applications are decided immediately under each Chama’s configured rules. It is read-only and does not reconcile bank or M-PESA statements or move money.

    Can our group chat inside the app?

    Yes. Active accounts can use the chama's in-app group chat. It refreshes through the event stream with polling and reconnection support. WhatsApp/SMS broadcasts and chat by email are not included.

    Can we correct a contribution date after recording it?

    Only the Chairperson or Treasurer can correct the date of a confirmed cash contribution, and a non-empty reason is required. The new date must be valid, not future-dated, stay in the original month, and preserve whether the contribution was on time or late. Amount, member, and other transaction fields are unchanged. An audit event records the actor, time, old and new dates, and reason; the audit store is not tamper-evident.

    Does Jirani Chama receive, verify, or move our money?

    No. It records cash already received or paid outside the app. It does not collect or disburse funds, and bank/M-PESA settlement matching is not enabled.

    Which plan fits our group?

    Basic supports up to 10 members. Growth Chama supports up to 30 members. Enterprise / Investment is for groups above 30 members and is arranged with sales. Member limits are enforced by the server when members are invited or activated.

    How does the annual discount work?

    Basic is KSh 2,880 per year instead of KSh 3,600, saving KSh 720. Growth Chama is KSh 5,760 per year instead of KSh 7,200, saving KSh 1,440. Both annual options are 20% off the equivalent monthly total.

    When does payment confirm our subscription?

    A subscription becomes active only after the server verifies a real provider transaction for the expected KES amount and the group’s reference. Clicking a plan or reporting that you paid never activates access. Online payment collection is not enabled yet.

    What happens when a subscription expires?

    Your financial history remains available to view and export. New financial records, member invitations, loan decisions, and other subscription-gated actions are blocked for unpaid or expired groups. Online payments are not enabled yet; contact us about pricing and renewal.
