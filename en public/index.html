Security

Built for Trust and Accountability

The platform keeps sensitive treasury and member data visible only where it is needed.

  • Role-scoped access. Officers and members can see only the parts of the chama they are meant to manage.
  • Two-step approvals. Sensitive changes like leadership and loans require review and confirmation before they take effect.
  • Audit clarity. Every action is recorded so the group can trace who approved, changed, or recorded each event.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jirani Chama?

A modern financial management platform designed for Kenyan table-banking and savings groups to keep contributions, loans, and decisions in one secure space.

How do I get my group started?

Register your profile with a private PIN, use direct invitation codes to invite members, assign officer roles, and start tracking records instantly.

Can we track individual contributions and balances?

Yes. The Group Treasury features real-time pooled balance tracking and instant visibility into individual member ledgers.

Are our group's money and data secure?

Yes. We use role-scoped access, two-step approvals for sensitive changes (like loans), and a tamper-evident audit trail for complete transparency.

How are monthly contributions split between welfare and share capital?

By default, the first monthly contribution payment is applied toward the KES 500 welfare target, and any remaining amount is allocated to share capital. We also supports proportional and custom allocation rules when registering your chama.

How does the platform handle loan approvals and repayments?

Loans require three role-based approvals and support 1, 2, 3, or 6 month terms with reducing-balance or flat interest models. The current monthly interest rate is 5%. Repayments clear interest first, followed by principal, starting with the earliest installment.

Can we automatically enforce late payment penalties?

Yes. After the monthly deadline, the platform can assess one outstanding KES 200 penalty for an active member who has not reached the KES 500 contribution target. The default deadline is the 5th day of the month, and a chama set their own day.
