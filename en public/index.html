    Officer assignments

    After setup, the Secretary proposes officer candidates. Eligible group accounts vote by secret ballot; the Chairperson opens and closes voting and verifies the result. Verified changes take effect after 24 hours.

    Leadership elections

    The Chairperson or Secretary schedules candidates and a date. Eligible chama accounts can cast one secret vote on that date only, from midnight to 11:59 p.m. Nairobi time. The highest vote count wins and takes office when voting closes; a tie requires a scheduled runoff.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Jirani Chama?

    A modern financial management platform designed for Kenyan table-banking and savings groups to keep contributions, loans, and decisions in one secure space.

    How do I get my group started?

    Register your profile with a private PIN, use direct invitation codes to invite members, assign officer roles, and start tracking records instantly.

    Can we track individual contributions and balances?

    Yes. Members and officers can review recorded contributions, balances, loans, and repayments. Money is still received and paid outside the app.

    Are our group's money and data secure?

    Accounts use private PINs and role-based permissions. Important changes are recorded so officers can review group activity.

    How are monthly contributions split between welfare and share capital?

    The chairperson sets the monthly welfare amount during chama registration. Each member's first contribution payment of the month covers that amount, and any remainder is allocated to share capital.

    How does the platform handle loan approvals and repayments?

    Loans require three role-based approvals and support 1, 2, 3, or 6 month terms with reducing-balance or flat interest models. The monthly interest rate defaults to 5% and can be changed during chama registration. Repayments clear interest first, followed by principal, starting with the earliest installment.

    How are late-payment penalties assessed?

    After the configured deadline, the Chairperson or Secretary runs the penalty assessment. The app does not assess penalties automatically. At most one outstanding penalty is assessed for an eligible member and month.

    Which plan fits our group?

    Basic supports up to 10 members. Growth Chama supports up to 30 members. Enterprise / Investment is for groups above 30 members and is arranged with sales. Member limits are enforced by the server when members are invited or activated.

    How does the annual discount work?

    Basic is KSh 2,880 per year instead of KSh 3,600, saving KSh 720. Growth Chama is KSh 5,760 per year instead of KSh 7,200, saving KSh 1,440. Both annual options are 20% off the equivalent monthly total.

    When does payment confirm our subscription?

    A subscription becomes active only after the server verifies a real provider transaction for the expected KES amount and the group’s reference. Clicking a plan or reporting that you paid never activates access. Online payment collection is not enabled yet.

    What happens when a subscription expires?

    Your financial history remains available to view and export. New financial records, member invitations, loan decisions, and other subscription-gated actions are blocked for unpaid or expired groups. Online payments are not enabled yet; contact us about pricing and renewal.
