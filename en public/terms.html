2.2 The Platform does not hold, transfer, lend, disburse or receive member funds. All money belonging to a Chama is held in accounts that the Chama controls, such as its bank or mobile money accounts. Every cash movement between members and the Chama occurs outside the Platform. 2.3 Loans are made from the pooled savings of the members of each Chama, in accordance with that Chama's constitution and rules. We do not provide loan funds and are not a lender, deposit-taker or credit provider.

5. Contributions, penalties and repayments

5.1 Contributions, penalties and repayments recorded on the Platform are records of cash received or owed. Allocation of payments follows the order set by your Chama's rules, which by default apply outstanding penalties and welfare contributions before share capital.

5.2 Cash-out entries cannot be recorded on the Platform. Withdrawals from a Chama are made outside the Platform under the Chama's rules.

5.3 An authorised officer may correct the date of a contribution entry only within the same calendar month as the original entry. Every correction must include a reason and is recorded in the audit log.

6. Loans

6.1 Each Chama sets its own loan rules, which may include: loan amount tiers; minimum membership period; minimum share contributions; limits on total loans outstanding; limits relative to a borrower's contributions and existing principal; and guarantor requirements. The Platform applies these rules automatically to each loan application.

6.2 Loan eligibility is decided automatically by the Platform using these rules. Where the Platform declines or limits a loan, it records the reasons.

6.3 A borrower must have at least two active members, other than the borrower, to act as guarantors. Each guarantor must have sufficient unpledged share capital to cover the guaranteed amount, and that share capital is held for the duration of the loan under the Chama's rules.

6.4 Repayment schedules are calculated using a reducing-balance method over a term of 1, 2, 3 or 6 months, at the interest rate the Chama has configured. The default monthly rate shown at setup is 5%, which the Chama may change within its own rules. Interest is payable into the Chama's savings pool, not to us.

6.5 Human review. A borrower may ask for a review of an automated loan decision. The review is carried out by an authorised officer with no conflict of interest in the application. The reviewer records a written finding (upheld or not upheld) and reasons, which the borrower can see. A review finding does not, by itself, change loan status, funding, balances, guarantor holds or accounting entries. Any such change happens only if the Chama acts on the finding under its own rules.

6.6 A Chama is responsible for ensuring that its loan rules, interest rates and penalties are lawful and enforceable under Kenyan law, including the Law of Contract Act (Cap. 23) and the Consumer Protection Act, 2012.

7. Penalties and fines

7.1 Late payment penalties and other fines are set by each Chama and recorded on the Platform. The default late penalty shown at setup is KES 200 per late contribution.

7.2 A penalty must be reasonable, proportionate and set in a manner that complies with Kenyan law. A Chama that imposes a penalty that a court finds unenforceable bears the consequences of that finding.

8. Dividends

8.2 Committing a dividend on the Platform adds the allocated amount to members' recorded share capital. Committing a dividend does not transfer cash. Any cash payment of dividends happens outside the Platform under the Chama's rules.
