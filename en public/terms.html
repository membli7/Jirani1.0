Terms

Jirani Chama helps groups record their contributions, loans, approvals, and decisions. It does not move money or provide financial advice.

Prices shown are informational. The app does not initiate STK pushes, payment requests, or live subscription charges. The M-PESA C2B subscription verifier is sandbox-only and remains disabled until public HTTPS validation and confirmation URLs are registered and the shortcode-to-group map is configured. Choosing a plan or reporting a payment does not start a charge or activate access.

When sandbox callbacks are configured, a subscription becomes active only after the server accepts a callback from Safaricom's published callback IPs and matches the pending plan's exact KES amount, shortcode, group reference, and unique receipt. Production billing is not enabled and would require separate configuration and authorization.

Groups with unpaid or expired subscriptions retain view access to existing financial history and exports. New subscription-gated actions remain unavailable until a verified renewal.
